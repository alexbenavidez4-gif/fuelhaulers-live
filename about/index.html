About fuelhaulers.live

fuelhaulers.live is a free website and phone app for fuel haulers and tanker drivers. Drivers at the rack post how busy each fuel terminal is right now, from dead to packed, with lanes down, product outages, and about how long the wait is.

Built by a working fuel hauler in San Antonio, Texas, for other fuel haulers. It covers 129 fuel racks and terminals across Texas and Utah. Free. No ads to click through, nothing to buy.

What you can do

Live rack reports

Drivers tap how busy a terminal is right now (dead, moderate, busy, or packed), plus lanes down, product outages, and the wait time. Each report shows how old it is.

Usual busy hours

Every terminal has a chart of its usual busy and quiet hours for each day of the week, built from past driver reports, so you can plan when to load.

HOS Recap calculator

A free 70-hour / 8-day recap calculator. It shows hours available, hours dropping off at midnight, and handles 34-hour restarts. Your hours stay on your phone.

City chat

Chat with other drivers in your area about racks, roads, and the job.

Scale reports

Drivers report whether weigh stations are open or closed.

Jobs and carrier reviews

Fuel hauling job listings and driver reviews of carriers.

Private messages

Message other drivers directly, with a block button if you need it.

Install like an app

Add fuelhaulers.live to your home screen on Android or iPhone. No app store needed.

Questions about fuelhaulers.live

What is fuelhaulers.live?

A free site and phone app where fuel haulers share live fuel rack and terminal wait times, plus a free HOS recap calculator, city chat, scale reports, and fuel hauling jobs.

Is fuelhaulers.live free?

Yes. Reading reports and using the HOS Recap calculator are free with no account. A free account is only needed to post rack reports, chat, or message drivers.

Where do the rack wait times come from?

From drivers who are at the rack. Each report shows how busy the terminal is, lanes down, product outages, the wait time, and how long ago it was posted. When nobody has posted recently, the terminal shows its usual busy hours from past reports.

Which fuel terminals does it cover?

129 fuel racks and terminals across Texas and Utah, including San Antonio, Houston, Dallas, Austin, Corpus Christi, El Paso, the Permian Basin, and Salt Lake City.

Can I use it on my phone like an app?

Yes. Open fuelhaulers.live on your phone and tap Install. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. There is nothing to download from an app store.

Does the HOS Recap calculator save my hours online?

No. Your hours save only on your own phone. Nothing is sent to fuelhaulers.live or anyone else.
