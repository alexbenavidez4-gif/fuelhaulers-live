fuelhaulers.live: live fuel rack wait times for fuel haulers

Know the Rack before you roll!

Live fuel rack wait times, from the drivers in line

fuelhaulers.live is a free site and app for fuel haulers and tanker drivers. Drivers at the rack post how busy each fuel terminal is right now, from dead to packed, with lanes down, product outages, and about how long the wait is. Check the rack before you roll, see the usual busy hours for each terminal, and pick the fastest load.

It also has city chat for 20 Texas and Utah areas, weigh station (scale) reports, fuel hauling jobs, carrier reviews, private messages between drivers, and a free 70-hour / 8-day HOS recap calculator. Built by fuel haulers, for fuel haulers.

New to the rules? Read Texas vs. federal hours of service, how the 70-hour recap works, and the 34-hour restart.
